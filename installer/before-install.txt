Chromebook SOF Audio (open)
===========================

Open Windows 10/11 audio drivers for Intel Gemini Lake Chromebooks
(Celeron N4000/N4020/N4100/N4120, Pentium Silver N5000/N5030) running
MrChromebox UEFI firmware, with the DA7219 headset codec and MAX98357A
speaker amp (Google "Octopus" boards, e.g. Acer Chromebook 311 "Bobba",
HP Chromebook 14 G6 "Dorp").

Speakers, headphones, the headset mic and the built-in mics work, with
automatic switching when a headset is plugged in or out.

Before you continue:

- The drivers are TEST-SIGNED. The installer turns on Windows test signing
  (bcdedit /set testsigning on) and trusts this project's test certificate.
  Windows then shows "Test Mode" in a corner of the desktop.
- Secure Boot must be OFF in the firmware setup, or test-signed drivers
  cannot load. The installer stops without changing anything if it is on.
- If CoolStar's audio driver is installed, this installer takes over the
  audio DSP device. Uninstalling this program gives it back.
- Restart when the installer asks you to.

CoolStar. Use at your own risk.
